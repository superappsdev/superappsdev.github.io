Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Baby Heartbeat Monitor at Home mobile application and related services operated by superappsdev (“Service Provider”, “we”, “us”).

Effective date: 27 July 2026

Summary

  • No account is required. We do not ask you for your name, email, or similar details inside the app.
  • Recordings, notes, and favorites are stored on your device only — not on our servers.
  • We use Google services (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics) that may collect device identifiers and usage or diagnostic data.
  • The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not a medical device.

Information Collection and Use

Data you create in the app (stored on your device)

When you use the Application, the following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Audio recordings captured with your microphone
  • Recording names, personal notes, and favorite markers
  • Basic in-app preferences (for example, whether you have seen guidance tips)

This information is not uploaded to the Service Provider’s servers. If you use your device’s share feature, you choose where to send a recording; we do not receive a copy.

Data collected automatically (third-party SDKs)

The Application uses third-party services that may automatically collect limited technical data when you use the Application, including:

  • Device or other identifiers (such as an advertising ID)
  • App interactions and usage events (for example, screens viewed and feature use)
  • App performance and diagnostic information (such as crash logs and diagnostics)
  • Information related to ad delivery and interaction with ads

This data is processed by Google and its service providers. It is used for advertising, analytics, and app stability as described in our Google Play Data safety disclosure.

Microphone

The Application uses your device microphone only when you start a recording. Recordings remain on your device unless you share them through your device’s share menu.

Not a Medical Device

The Application is for personal listening, bonding, and keeping memories. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, fetal heart-rate measurement, or medical monitoring. Do not use the Application to make medical decisions.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics)

We do not control how these third parties collect, use, or retain data. Please review their policies for more information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Application and its third-party SDKs may use SDKs, device identifiers, and similar technologies to support advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and service delivery. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

How We Use Information

We use information as follows:

  • On-device data: to let you record, play back, organize, and share recordings from your device
  • Third-party SDK data: to display ads, understand how the Application is used, improve stability, and fix crashes

We do not sell your recordings. We do not operate user accounts and do not store your recordings on our servers.

Data Retention

  • On your device: Recordings, notes, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the Application, clear the Application’s storage, or uninstall the Application.
  • Third-party SDK data: Retained according to Google’s and its providers’ policies. We do not control those retention periods.

Data Deletion

On your device: Delete recordings in the app, or uninstall the app / clear its storage to remove all local data.

Ads and analytics: Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device’s privacy or ad settings. Google-processed data is governed by Google’s policies.

We do not store your recordings on our servers, so there is usually nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Advertising partners such as Google AdMob may process device identifiers for advertising purposes.

International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, appropriate mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses or other legally recognized transfer tools may be used by those providers.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we process. Data transmitted by third-party SDKs is typically encrypted in transit. Recordings you create remain on your device and are your responsibility to safeguard.

Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that affects personal data we control and applicable law requires notification, we will notify affected users in accordance with legal requirements.

Children

The Application is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Opt-Out

Uninstalling the Application stops further on-device data collection and removes locally stored recordings and notes. It does not delete data already processed by third-party providers; use your device ad settings and Google’s policies for that data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy with a new effective date. Where required by law, we will seek your consent before material changes take effect.
